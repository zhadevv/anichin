# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project expects everyone participating — in issues, pull requests,
discussions, or any other project space — to be respectful and
constructive.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what's best for the project and its users
- Being patient with newcomers and less experienced contributors

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (issues, PRs, discussions) and
when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior by opening a private security advisory (see
`SECURITY.md`) or contacting the maintainer directly. Reports will be
reviewed and may result in a warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban from
project spaces, at the maintainer's discretion, proportionate to the
severity of the behavior.

## Attribution

Adapted from the general structure of the Contributor Covenant, trimmed
to what's proportionate for a small open-source library maintained by a
single person.
